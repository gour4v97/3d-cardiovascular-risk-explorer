# Asset and licensing record

## Current Foundation-phase viewer

The current viewer creates a simple procedural heart form and LAD, LCX, and RCA tube geometry at runtime in `web/app.js`. It does not include or redistribute an external anatomical mesh, texture, image, or patient data.

## Before release

Replace the procedural heart only with a mesh whose source URL, creator, license, required attribution, and any redistribution/share-alike conditions are recorded in this file. Display the required credit in the application footer. Do not use a mesh with unclear licensing.
